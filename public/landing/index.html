[04] GAME MODE

        
Where's this camera?

        
A live frame from a real public traffic camera, somewhere on Earth. Captions redacted. Spin the globe, drop your pin, and score up to 5,000 points a round by how close you land. Five rounds.

      


      

        
01 · Watch
A live, unlabelled feed from one of thousands of public cameras.

        
02 · Pin
Read the roads, signs and skyline, then click the globe.

        
03 · Score
Up to 5,000 points by distance. Copy your result and share it.
